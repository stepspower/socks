# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-11

First release. SOCKS for your Tesla: a lean Linux desktop companion.

- Bluetooth-first: status, controls and history without any Tesla cloud account; key pairing from the app.
- Status tab with click-to-change values and slide switches (lock, sentry, charge port, frunk/trunk, climate, charging); no confirmation pop-ups.
- One-off **Depart by**: time, charge level and cabin temperature; the car does the timing, SOCKS clears it afterwards.
- Local SQLite history: drives, charging sessions, efficiency, battery health, learned pack size, places with automatic labels, one-click purpose, yearly km-register export, CSV/JSON/GPX/PNG/SVG exports.
- Optional Tesla Fleet API: remote access, trip watch while the car is away, Supercharger invoice PDFs.
- Setup wizard, six languages (en, nl, cs, de, nb, sv), rotating redacted log file, Bluetooth signal strength.
- Packages: Flatpak bundle, .deb, .rpm, wheel; Flathub submission pending.
